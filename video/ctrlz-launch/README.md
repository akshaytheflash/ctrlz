`ctrlz-launch.mp4` · 1920×1080 · 30 fps · 22.0 s · H.264 + AAC 48 kHz stereo · 9.3 MB

## Concept

**The shadow tree.** git protects commits; ctrlz keeps a shadow copy of the working tree
between them. One continuous take: a block caret opens into a terminal, the terminal pulls
back to reveal the working tree over its shadow store, an agent breaks it, and the shadow
comes back up and *becomes* the tree. The caret is the only hand; every boundary is carried
by it or by the shadow cards.

## Look

`look.json` → `ctrlz-phosphor`, taken from the product's own terminal palette:
ground `#0c111a`, ink `#f4f8ff`, phosphor `#5eead4`, failure `#ff7a7a`.
Faces: Geist (display) / Inter (text) / Geist Mono (terminal), subset and inlined by
`look.py apply` into `look.js`. Contrast check: ink 17.75:1, mute 7.23:1, accent 12.78:1.

## Sound

`score.py` reads the comp's `window.__events()` (56 events) and builds one room: wood ticks
for keys, glass for lands, sub for weight, air for moves, all through a 1.5 s impulse. A low
pad follows the story's seven sections, gated down in the stills and ducked under every
contact. 48 kHz stereo, peak −8.0 dBFS.

- `comp.html`: the film; every value is a pure function of `t` (`window.__seek`)
- `motion.js`: the move library the comp calls (`window.OM`)

- `sfx.wav`: the score; `score.py` + `events.json` regenerate it
